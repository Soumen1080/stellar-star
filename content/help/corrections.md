How you fix an expense depends on whether anyone has paid toward it yet.

## Nobody has paid yet

- **Edit** the expense to change its title, amount, split or members. If
  someone else edited it at the same time, you're shown both versions to
  merge before anything is saved.
- **Void** the expense to remove it from the trip's balances. Give a short
  reason (at least 3 characters); it is kept in the expense's audit history.

The payer, the creator or a trip organizer can void an expense.

## Someone has already paid

Payments on the Stellar network cannot be undone, so a settled or partly
settled expense cannot be edited in place or voided. Add a **linked
correction** instead:

- **Reversal** offsets the original expense completely.
- **Adjustment** changes the effective total, for example when the bill was
  entered with the wrong amount.

The original expense and its payments stay visible, and the correction is
linked to them. Balances are recalculated from both.

> A correction changes what people owe from now on. It does not send money
> back. If someone overpaid, they settle the difference with a new payment.

## When there's a disagreement

If members disagree about an expense, raise a dispute on it. Settlement for
that expense is paused until the dispute is resolved. For how payments and
disputes relate, see [Pay your share](/help/payments#disputes).
