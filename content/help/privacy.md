Stellar-star keeps two kinds of data, and they follow different rules.

## Public on the Stellar network

Payments you sign are written to the public Stellar ledger: account
addresses, amounts, assets, transaction hashes and timestamps. Nobody,
including Stellar-star, can edit or delete them. The app never writes your
name, email or other personal details into a transaction.

## Stored by Stellar-star

Trip and expense details, display names, contacts, invitations and receipt
attachments are stored off-chain. Access is limited to the members of each
trip. Some records are kept for a limited time only:

- **Receipt attachments**: up to 2 years, then removed automatically.
- **Retry records for payments and other actions**: the safe-retry summary
  for 24 hours, minimal audit facts for 30 days.

## Export your data

Open [Privacy](/privacy) while signed in and choose **Download Data Archive**
to download a copy of the off-chain data linked to your wallet.

## Delete your account

On the same page, choose **Request Account Deletion** and confirm. This erases or anonymizes your
off-chain data. Payments already on the Stellar network stay public there,
because the ledger can't be changed.
