Your wallet is the only thing that can move your money. These habits keep it
that way.

## Never share your secret key or recovery phrase

Stellar-star, its staff and its support will **never** ask for your secret
key (it starts with `S`) or your recovery phrase. Anyone who asks is trying
to steal from you, even if they seem to be helping.

## Check every signature request

Before you approve anything in your wallet:

1. **Network**: it matches the app (shown in the footer).
2. **Destination**: it's the person you mean to pay. Compare the first and
   last characters of the address.
3. **Amount and asset**: they match the share shown in the app.

Signing in only asks you to sign a challenge message. If a sign-in prompt
asks you to approve a payment, reject it.

## Treat invite links like keys

Anyone with an invite link can claim that slot in your trip. Send links
privately, and revoke any link you shared by mistake
([how](/help/invitations)).

## Watch for impersonation

- Check the address bar before connecting your wallet. Bookmark the real
  site and use the bookmark.
- Real problems with the service are posted on the [status page](/status).
  Don't trust "urgent" messages that tell you to move funds.
- Nobody can "unlock", "verify" or "recover" a payment for a fee.

## If something looks wrong

Stop, don't sign, and check the [status page](/status). To report a
suspected scam that uses Stellar-star's name, email
[privacy@stellarstar.app](mailto:privacy@stellarstar.app) with what you saw.
Leave out your keys and recovery phrase.
