You can add friends to a trip before they have a wallet. Each one is a
placeholder member whose share is tracked exactly. They take over their
place later by claiming it with an invite link.

## Create an invite link

1. Open the trip and choose **Invite Friend** in the member list.
2. Pick the member slot the link is for, or a general group invite.
3. Choose **Generate Invitation Link** and send it to that person privately.

An invite link from the invite dialog works **once** and **expires after 7
days**. If the request is retried because of a dropped connection, you still
get one link, not two.

> An invite link is a key to your trip. Send it only to the person it is for,
> and never post it publicly.

## Join with a link

1. Open the link and connect your wallet ([how](/help/wallet-setup)).
2. Check the trip name and pick your slot if you're asked to.
3. Choose **Join Trip**. Your wallet address now belongs to that member, and any
   shares already assigned to them are yours.

A slot can be claimed only once. If two people try to claim the same slot at
the same moment, exactly one of them gets it.

## When a link doesn't work

- **Expired, revoked or already used**: ask the person who invited you for a
  new link. They can revoke the old one at any time.
- **Slot already claimed**: someone else holds that place. Ask the organizer
  to check the member list.
- **Wallet already a member**: this wallet is already in the trip. Open the
  trip from your [dashboard](/dashboard).

## Placeholder members and paying

A member without a wallet never blocks everyone else. Members with wallets
can still settle among themselves while that person's share waits for them to
claim it.
